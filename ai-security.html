FAQ

Common questions

What is prompt injection?

Prompt injection is an attack where malicious instructions, typed directly or hidden in documents, emails or web pages, trick an AI system into ignoring its rules, leaking data or taking unwanted actions. It is ranked LLM01 in the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications.

Is my small business really a target?

Yes. Any business whose chatbot or AI assistant reads outside content or can take actions is exposed, and automated attacks do not check company size.

What do you test?

Chatbots, AI agents, document-processing pipelines and any LLM feature connected to your data, tools or customers, including direct and indirect prompt injection, data leakage and tool abuse.

What do I get at the end?

A prioritized report with clear findings, proof of how each issue was reproduced and practical fixes. We can also implement the fixes and add monitoring.
